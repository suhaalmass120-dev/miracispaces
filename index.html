One team from the first sketch to the day you move in.

Miraci Spaces is a design and build company in Mangaluru. Architecture, construction and interiors sit with one team, so the decisions made on the drawing board are the ones you live with.

Homes on this coast face heavy monsoons, salt air and humid heat. We start from that climate: deep overhangs for shade and rain, stone and timber chosen for the weather, and glass placed for the breeze and the view.

What we do

Site studies, planning, elevations and the drawings needed for approvals, shaped around how your family lives and how your plot sits.

Structure, stonework, glazing and finishes managed by the people who drew them, with one point of contact until handover.

Layouts, lighting, joinery and furnishing planned alongside the architecture, so the inside and outside read as one home.

How a home comes together

  1. Brief

    We visit your plot and listen to how you want to live.

  2. Concept design

    Plans and views you can react to before anything is fixed.

  3. Approvals

    Drawings and paperwork for the required permissions.

  4. Construction

    Building on site, with regular updates on progress.

  5. Interiors

    Joinery, lighting and furnishing brought in as one plan.

  6. Handover

    A final walk through together, and the keys are yours.
